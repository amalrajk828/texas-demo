## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-precision visual language for a premier industrial engineering and process automation firm operating across Kuwait and Dubai. Tailored for enterprise energy operators, procurement directors, and technical plant managers, the aesthetic projects ISO-certified rigor, engineering gravitas, and forward-looking technological fluency.

The visual direction marries **Corporate Modernism** with controlled **Industrial Glassmorphism**:
- Grounded, high-clarity foundational layouts built on clean light neutrals and soft slate-blue atmospheric layers.
- Tactile, frosted-glass card surfaces layered above ambient chromatic glowing nodes to signify advanced telemetry, flow dynamics, and automation.
- Authoritative deep brick red accents evoking precision instrumentation, structural steel, and critical process control.
- Dark structural fixtures (the floating pill navigation bar and deep base footer) bookending the experience to communicate mechanical stability and enterprise strength.

## Colors

The palette balances technical discipline with enterprise energy presence. 

- **Primary (`#8a302f`)**: Deep brick red used for critical calls-to-action, key telemetry badges, active indicators, and high-impact structural highlights. Its hover counterpart (`#c0483f`) injects energetic focus on interaction.
- **Secondary (`#16202b`)**: Deep solid dark navy applied exclusively to all primary headers and structural emphasis. Typography remains purely solid, rejecting gradients or faded fills to preserve legibility and institutional authority.
- **Tertiary (`#eef3f8` / `#e7f0f7`)**: Cool, desaturated technical light-blue tints used to create rhythmic alternating background sections without visual fatigue.
- **Neutral (`#4a5568`)**: Slate graphite engineered for effortless long-form reading across technical specifications and case studies, complemented by muted cool slate (`#8a94a3`) for metadata, sub-labels, and captions.
- **Structural Anchors**: The header employs a floating dark glass pill (`rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85)`) to isolate navigation, while the foundation rests on ultra-deep dark obsidian (`#0a0b0e`).

## Typography

The typography pairs the geometric authority of **Sora** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

- **Headlines (Sora)**: Conveys technical architecture, structural balance, and modern engineering excellence. All headings must be rendered in solid `#16202b` to project institutional trust. Character spacing stays slightly tight on large displays to enhance graphic firmness.
- **Body & Data (Inter)**: Delivers neutral, highly legible execution across dense technical documentation, flow charts, operational metrics, and spec sheets.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Sora)**: Applied to UI buttons, status flags, and ISO certification tags to provide crisp mechanical clarity at small optical sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm using a 12-column responsive fluid grid maxed at 1320px for desktop environments:
- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, with 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, with 32px (`2rem`) page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, with 20px (`1.25rem`) page margins.

Alternating background bands structure the page pacing: pristine pure white (`#ffffff`) for focal case studies and deep dives, followed by soft technical light-blue bands (`#eef3f8` and `#e7f0f7`) for service matrices and capability grids. Section paddings range between 80px and 120px to ensure an expansive, calm enterprise rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined glassmorphic approach supported by ambient chromatic glows:

1. **Card Depth (Glassmorphism)**: Primary feature and service containers are rendered in translucent white `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)` accompanied by `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.3)`. Borders are framed with a precise 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)` and layered with an ultra-soft dual shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 32, 43, 0.04), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(22, 32, 43, 0.06)`.
2. **Atmospheric Glowing Blobs**: Organic blurred radial gradients (`rgba(192, 72, 63, 0.12)` for warm instrumentation nodes, and `rgba(30, 77, 122, 0.14)` for cool telemetry nodes) sit behind the glass cards. They diffuse softly through the frosted surfaces to produce depth without compromising legibility.
3. **Floating Dark Navigation**: Positioned fixed top-center, the navbar is composed as a dark glass pill in `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle interior border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, floating effortlessly over both light and tinted backgrounds.
4. **Foundation Footer**: Solid `#0a0b0e` surface anchoring the page with no blur, conveying mechanical stability and permanence.

## Shapes

The design balances precision engineering with modern softness:
- **Glassmorphic Cards**: Standardized at a generous `24px` radius (`1.5rem`), creating an approachable, high-end container for dense process flows.
- **Interactive Controls**: Inputs, chips, and small modules utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner rounding for crisp functional alignment.
- **Navigation & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for the floating header, status badges, and pill-variant action buttons, contrasting with the structural grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#8a302f` fill, pure white `#ffffff` text (Sora `label-lg`), 8px border radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover transitions smoothly to `#c0483f` with a soft elevation lift (`0 8px 16px rgba(138, 48, 47, 0.20)`).
- **Secondary / Technical**: Outlined with 1.5px solid `#16202b`, text in `#16202b`. On hover, fills with `#16202b` and transitions text to `#ffffff`.
- **Glass / Tertiary**: Frosted translucent surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)` with a 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`, text in `#16202b`.

### Glass Cards (Services & Custody Metering)
- Constructed with `24px` rounded corners, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.3)`, and a crisp 1px perimeter border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`.
- Internal padding: `space-xl` (40px desktop, 24px mobile).
- Ambient glowing red/blue vector orbs are placed directly beneath these card coordinates to create the signature industrial glow.

### Navigation Pill
- Centered floating layout constrained to `max-width: 1040px`, suspended 20px below the viewport ceiling.
- Surface: `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop blur, fully rounded (`9999px`) pill profile, bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Links in Sora `14px` weight 500, muted silver-white `#cbd5e1`, shifting to `#ffffff` and accented with small `#c0483f` indicator dots when active.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Fields
- Background: Solid `#ffffff` or semi-opaque `#ffffff` with 1.5px border `#e2e8f0`.
- 8px border radius, text in Inter `15px` (`#16202b`), placeholder in `#8a94a3`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#8a302f` paired with a 3px outer glow ring `rgba(138, 48, 47, 0.15)`.

### Chips & ISO Badges
- Pill-shaped badges using soft tinted fills: `#eef3f8` surface with `#16202b` text for specifications, and `#fdf2f2` surface with `#8a302f` text for compliance certifications (e.g., "ISO 9001:2015").
- 1px matching border with 4px 12px padding and Sora `label-md` uppercase styling.

### Technical Metrics & Inspection Counters
- Monospace/Sora numeral pairings styled in deep `#16202b` with a small red unit affix (`#8a302f`), supported below by uppercase caption labels in `#8a94a3`.
